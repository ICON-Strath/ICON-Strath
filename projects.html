ACTIVE PROJECT

Investigation of Model Predictive Control in SiC-Based Modular Multilevel Converter

Principal Investigator: Dr Xin Yuan

This project investigates a novel model predictive control strategy for SiC-based half-bridge modular multilevel converters, with particular emphasis on reducing the parameter sensitivity of model predictive control.

The research targets medium-voltage motor-drive applications and aims to contribute to improved energy efficiency, reduced system cost, and improved environmental sustainability through advanced converter control and wide-bandgap power electronics.
