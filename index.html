WHAT WE DO

Research Areas

01

EV & Aerospace Motor Control

Advanced electrical machine drives and control technologies for future transportation and electrified propulsion systems.

02

Modular Multilevel Converters

Advanced MMC topologies, modulation, control, and high-power-density converter technologies for motor-drive applications.

03

Wide-Bandgap Power Electronics

High-frequency and high-power-density converter technologies based on GaN and SiC devices.

04

Advanced Control

Predictive, sensorless, model-free and intelligent control for electrical machines and power electronic systems.

OUR UNIVERSITY

University of Strathclyde

The University of Strathclyde is located in Glasgow, the largest city in Scotland. Its Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering is one of the oldest and largest of its kind in the UK.

The research group is part of the Institute for Energy and Environment, with internationally recognised research in power systems, power electronics, electrical machines, and sustainable energy technologies.

The Department and Institute maintain extensive international collaborations with universities and research institutions worldwide.
