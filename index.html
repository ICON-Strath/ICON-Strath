University of Strathclyde

The University of Strathclyde is located in Glasgow, the largest city in Scotland. Its Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering is one of the long-established and major electrical engineering departments in the UK.

The research group is part of the Institute for Energy and Environment, one of Europe’s leading and largest university research groups in power systems and energy technologies, with internationally recognised research in power systems, power electronics, and sustainable energy.

The Department maintains extensive international academic collaborations, including long-term partnerships with universities in China.
