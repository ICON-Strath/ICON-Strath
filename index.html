OUR ENVIRONMENT

Based at the University of Strathclyde

The ICON Research Team is based in the Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering at the University of Strathclyde in Glasgow, UK.

Our research environment brings together power electronics, electrical machines, energy systems, advanced control, and strong industrial collaboration.
