WHAT WE DO

Research Areas

01

EV & Aerospace Motor Control

Advanced electrical machine drives and control technologies for future transportation and electrified propulsion systems.

02

Modular Multilevel Converters

Advanced MMC topologies, modulation, control, energy management, and high-power-density converter technologies.

03

Wide-Bandgap Power Electronics

High-frequency and high-power-density converter technologies based on GaN and SiC semiconductor devices.

04

Advanced Control

Predictive, sensorless, model-free, grid-forming, and intelligent control for electrical machines and power electronic systems.

OUR UNIVERSITY

University of Strathclyde

The University of Strathclyde is located in Glasgow, the largest city in Scotland. Its Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering is one of the oldest and largest of its kind in the UK.

The Department provides a strong research environment in power electronics, electrical machines, power systems, energy conversion, and advanced control.

RESEARCH ENVIRONMENT

Institute for Energy and Environment

The research group is part of the Institute for Energy and Environment, one of the major power and energy research institutes in Europe, with internationally recognised research in power systems, power electronics, electrical machines, and sustainable energy technologies.

The Institute maintains extensive international collaborations with universities and research institutions, supporting joint research, researcher exchange, and collaborative development in advanced energy and power-electronic technologies.
