Research

Advanced power electronics and drive technologies for transportation electrification.

Depending on the resources used to generate electricity, electrification can reduce carbon dioxide emissions from transportation, buildings and industrial sectors.

Our team focuses on the electrification of transportation applications, including electric-vehicle and aerospace motor drives, wireless power transfer, DC–DC converters, power converter hardware and advanced control.

Our long-term goal is to develop highly efficient, renewable and sustainable energy technologies that contribute to reducing dependence on fossil fuels.
