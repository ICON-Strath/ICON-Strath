Research

Power electronics and drive technologies for sustainable electrification.

Research Vision

Depending on the resources used to generate electricity, electrification can reduce carbon dioxide (CO₂) emissions from the transportation, building, and industrial sectors. Addressing emissions from these sectors is critical to decarbonizing the economy and, ultimately, mitigating the impacts of climate change.

Our team aims to develop electrification technologies for transportation applications, including EV and aerospace motor control, wireless power transfer, DC–DC converters, and related power electronic systems.

The long-term goal of our team is to develop highly efficient, renewable, and sustainable energy technologies that can contribute to replacing conventional fossil-fuel-based energy sources, including coal, oil, and natural gas.
