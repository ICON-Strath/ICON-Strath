FACILITIES · VER 16

DER-IC Scotland

The Driving the Electric Revolution Industrialisation Centres (DER-IC) equipment in Scotland is housed across two industry-led research and development facilities: PNDC and the National Manufacturing Institute Scotland (NMIS), both part of the University of Strathclyde.

Together, these facilities provide advanced capabilities for the development, testing, validation, manufacturing, and industrialisation of next-generation power electronics, electrical machines, drives, and energy technologies.

MW-SCALE TESTING & VALIDATION

PNDC / ANZIC

PNDC and ANZIC specialise in de-risking and accelerating the commercialisation of innovative MW-scale technologies and architectures through advanced testing, validation, real-world hardware, and simulation capabilities.

The facilities support technology development from component and subsystem level through to complete system verification, working across industry, academia, and policy-making organisations.

MW-Rated Dynamometers

Two 1 MW dynamometer systems with switchable torque and speed ranges and comprehensive data-acquisition capability for testing aviation, maritime, rail, and heavy-duty vehicle powertrains.

MW-Scale AC & DC Power Supplies

1 MVA four-quadrant power supplies capable of AC and DC operation, including three-phase and single-phase AC operation, with combined capability of up to approximately 2 MW and 3 kV DC.

Power Hardware-in-the-Loop

Multi-MW Power Hardware-in-the-Loop capability enables realistic system-level validation, including controlled electrical fault testing.

Environmental Testing

Environmental chamber testing can be combined with significant power hardware to evaluate power electronics, machines, and drives under controlled temperature and humidity conditions.

Multi-MW Energy Systems

The facilities complement PNDC's existing multi-MW whole-system capabilities across power, heat, and transport applications.

Hydrogen Infrastructure

Gaseous hydrogen supply supports testing and development for power, heat, transport, aerospace, rail, and heavy-duty vehicle applications.

ADVANCED MANUFACTURING & INDUSTRIALISATION

National Manufacturing Institute Scotland

The National Manufacturing Institute Scotland (NMIS) is an innovation partner for manufacturers, supporting industry in developing, scaling, and industrialising advanced technologies.

NMIS works with industrial and academic partners to accelerate manufacturing innovation, improve productivity, reduce development risk, and support the transition towards more sustainable manufacturing.

Its expanding semiconductor packaging capability provides manufacturers with access to advanced packaging technologies, supporting the development and industrialisation of future power electronic systems and semiconductor-based products.

INDUSTRIAL RESEARCH PARTNERSHIP

Rolls-Royce University Technology Centre in Electrical Power Systems

Established in 1997, the Rolls-Royce University Technology Centre in Electrical Power Systems conducts research and development into advanced electrical power-system technologies.

The Centre provides strategic and applied research supporting Rolls-Royce activities across aerospace, marine, energy, and electrical systems, strengthening the connection between fundamental research and industrial technology development.

Supporting the Energy Transition

The combined research infrastructure provides large-scale capabilities for technologies enabling the energy transition. This includes multi-MW electrical testing, power electronics and drive validation, environmental testing, advanced manufacturing, Power Hardware-in-the-Loop, and hydrogen infrastructure supporting the decarbonisation of aerospace, rail, heavy-duty transport, heat, and industrial energy systems.
